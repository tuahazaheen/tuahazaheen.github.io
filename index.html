About

I'm Tuaha, an Electrical Engineer currently working as a Science & Engineering Associate at United Bank Limited's Innovation & Creative Technology Digital Lab in Islamabad — where I move between QA/testing on iOS and Android builds, Figma UX/UI work, and microservices architecture.

My background started in hardware: RF validation and EMC/ESD testing at Rohde & Schwarz, and telecom power systems and optical transport networks at PTCL. My final year project, TwinWatt, built a digital-twin model for smart metering — the seed for the AI and research direction I've kept building on since.

I hold a BS in Electrical (Electronics) Engineering from Bahria University Islamabad. I'm especially drawn to work that sits between power engineering and applied AI, and I'm exploring international roles, particularly across the Gulf.
